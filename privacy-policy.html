Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 1, 2026

Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how DreamDay (“we”, “us”) collects, uses and protects the personal information you share with us when you use this website or book our wedding planning services.

Information We Collect

We collect the details you give us directly, such as your name, email address, phone number, wedding date and any message you send through our contact or newsletter forms.

We may also collect basic technical information, such as your browser type and pages visited, to help us improve the website.

How We Use Your Information

We use your information to reply to enquiries, plan and deliver the services you book, send newsletters you have subscribed to, and improve our website. We never sell your personal information.

Sharing Your Information

We only share your details with trusted vendors (for example venues, caterers or photographers) when this is needed to deliver your wedding, and only with your agreement.

Cookies

This website may use cookies to remember your preferences and understand how visitors use the site. You can turn cookies off in your browser settings at any time.

Your Rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, or unsubscribe from our newsletter at any time, by contacting us.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this policy, please contact us or email hello@example.com.
